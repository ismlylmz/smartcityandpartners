# EyeOnBlue ChildShield Demo
 
EyeOnBlue ChildShield is an open-source demo module for child-centred climate and health risk scoring and early warning.
 
The solution is designed to help local governments, schools, health facilities and community actors understand climate-related risks affecting children and take early action before harm occurs.
 
This repository has been prepared for the UNICEF Venture Fund Climate and Health 2026 application. It represents the proposed open-source module, not the full commercial EyeOnBlue platform.
 
## Problem
 
Children are highly vulnerable to climate-related hazards such as extreme heat, wildfire smoke, air pollution, floods, humidity, UV exposure and service disruptions. However, local authorities often lack simple, localized and child-centred tools to translate complex climate and environmental data into timely action.
 
In Antalya, Türkiye, schools, health facilities and communities are exposed to heatwaves, wildfires, extreme weather and flood-related risks. SCP is already working with local stakeholders on climate and disaster resilience, supported by a local environmental monitoring context and sensor network.
 
## Solution
 
EyeOnBlue ChildShield combines environmental indicators, satellite-derived risk layers, weather data, local sensor inputs, school and health facility locations, and aggregated demographic information to generate child-centred climate-health risk scores.
 
The system is designed to provide:
 
- School-level climate-health risk scores
- Health facility-level vulnerability indicators
- Community-level early warning signals
- Simple alerts for heat, air quality, wildfire smoke, flood and extreme weather risks
- Recommended early actions for local authorities, schools and health-related actors
 
## Data Privacy
 
ChildShield does not collect or process personal data from children.

The system uses:
 
- Environmental data
- Weather data
- Satellite-derived indicators
- Local sensor measurements
- Public school and health facility locations
- Aggregated demographic and statistical information
 
No names, identities, individual health records, personal locations or child-level personal data are required.
 
## Open-Source Scope
 
This repository includes the proposed open-source demo scope for the UNICEF Climate and Health 2026 application:
 
- Risk scoring concept
- Data schema
- Sample/demo data structure
- Basic risk scoring logic
- Example dashboard/API concept documentation
 
This repository does not include:
 
- EyeOnBlue’s commercial platform
- Proprietary production models
- Client-specific data
- Sensor integration credentials
- Internal infrastructure
- Commercial dashboards
- Confidential municipal or infrastructure operator data
 
## Pilot Context
 
The initial pilot context is Antalya, Türkiye.
 
The pilot will focus on generating climate-health risk scores and early warnings for schools, health facilities and communities exposed to:
 
- Extreme heat
- Wildfire smoke
- Air pollution
- Flood risk
- Extreme weather
- Service disruptions
 
## Intended Users
 
The intended users are:
 
- Municipalities
- Local governments
- Schools
- Health facilities
- Community leaders
- Public authorities working on climate adaptation, disaster resilience and child protection
 
## 12-Month Development Plan
 
The proposed 12-month plan includes:
 
1. Finalizing the open-source risk scoring methodology and data schema
2. Integrating satellite, weather, sensor, school and health facility layers
3. Developing a basic dashboard/API
4. Validating the early warning logic with local stakeholders

5. Testing alerts for heat, air quality, wildfire smoke, flood and extreme weather
6. Publishing documentation and preparing the module for replication in other cities and low-resource settings
